## CourtReady

Summary:
A civil court preparation assistant designed to help people understand their case, find legal aid, and prepare for hearings in accessible language.

What it does:
CourtReady guides users through plain-language intake, case classification, legal-aid routing, evidence preparation, and hearing practice. It supports English, Spanish, and Mandarin and is designed around accessibility and privacy.

Subashree's role:
Subashree worked with the CourtReady codebase and contributed to the user experience and routing timeline polish. The repository includes updates such as improving the routing timeline dots so all steps appear uniform and complete.

Main features:
- Voice-first intake
- Multilingual support in English, Spanish, and Mandarin
- Civil case classification and fact extraction
- Legal-aid organization routing
- Evidence checklist generation
- Hearing simulator with judge-style practice
- Printable preparation sheet
- Tone and confidence feedback
- Stateless privacy-focused design

Tools and technologies:
React, Vite, FastAPI, Python, JavaScript, CSS, Claude API, stateless JSON-based data.

What this project shows:
This project shows Subashree's interest in building socially useful tools, accessibility-focused interfaces, multilingual support, and AI-assisted workflows with careful ethical boundaries.
